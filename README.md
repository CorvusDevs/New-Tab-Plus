## What it is

New Tab Plus turns Safari's new tab into a private daily Flight Plan. Set one intention, move three useful things forward, open a focus block, and close the day with a short reflection. Supporting widgets and launchers stay available around that core routine.

## Features

- **A daily Flight Plan.** One calm timeline connects your morning intention, three priorities, a focus block, and an evening reflection.
- **A layout you control.** Drag any widget anywhere, resize it from its corner, save complete workspaces, and recover an earlier layout. Prefer the keyboard? Tab to a widget and use the arrow keys.
- **Clock and world clock.** Eleven clock faces, from analog to flip to word clock, plus a separate world clock for the cities you care about.
- **Weather at a glance.** Current conditions, how it actually feels outside, an hourly strip and a five day forecast.
- **Notes and to-do.** A scratchpad that understands Markdown, and a to-do list you can reorder by hand with due dates.
- **Shortcuts that learn.** Your most visited sites, filled in automatically and grouped however you like. Pin the ones that matter, hide the ones that do not.
- **Small tools, no tab required.** A timer and stopwatch, a pomodoro, a unit and currency converter, a calendar, quotes and RSS.
- **More than 60 backgrounds.** Animated scenes, curated space and nature photography, your own images, gradients, and time-of-day rotation.
- **Everything by keyboard.** Press Cmd+K to jump to a site, run a search, switch background, or toggle any widget.
- **Made to match you.** More than 20 distinct interface themes, accent colors, fonts, frosted or solid widgets, focus mode, and your own CSS if you want it.

## Privacy

**Your browsing stays on your Mac.** New Tab Plus has no account, no analytics and no telemetry. There is no server of ours for your data to reach, and none of it ever does.

To offer shortcuts that fill themselves in, the extension counts which sites you open, because Safari does not give extensions your history any other way. That counting happens on your Mac and is stored on your Mac. It records the address of a site and how often you open it, never the contents of your pages, and it is never transmitted.

You stay in control: pause the tracking, exclude any site, or clear everything at any time. Shortcuts you add by hand keep working with tracking off entirely. And one switch, **airplane mode**, stops every outbound connection the extension can make, including weather, feeds and the daily photo.

The [full privacy policy](https://corvusdevs.github.io/New-Tab-Plus/privacy.html) lists every connection the extension makes and exactly what each one receives.
